Where do you offer mobile detailing?+

Joshua serves North Jersey. Send your town or ZIP code by text to confirm service at your location.

How do I book an appointment?+

Choose a package using the booking button, then send your prepared text to Joshua at 862-777-0163. You can also call directly. Your appointment is confirmed once you agree on the details with Joshua.

Can I add stain or pet hair removal?+

Yes. Fabric stain extraction is $15 per seat, full floor extraction is $20, and extreme pet hair removal is $20. Add what you need to your estimate, then confirm the work with Joshua.

What should I have ready?+

When booking, share your vehicle type, location, and any areas that need extra attention. Joshua can confirm access, setup requirements, and timing for your appointment.
